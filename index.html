The Ground

No mirrors.
No machines.
Just earth.

WILD GYM was built by hand from reclaimed teak and volcanic stone, in a clearing between banyan trees and a rushing river. Every bar is wood you can feel, every weight is something you lift off the ground.

We train in small tribes, at the pace of the sun — first light sessions, midday shade sessions, and golden-hour flows. Humidity, birdsong and bare feet included.
